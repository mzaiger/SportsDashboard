"Closed" means the game is final. For Gemini's numbers, a push (spread margin lands exactly on the line) doesn't count toward or against ATS accuracy. The Confidence filter checks Gemini's own confidence score for the selected market (or either market, on "Both") — it only affects Gemini's numbers, never "My Accuracy" (a bet you made is graded the same regardless of how confident Gemini was). "Best Matchups" restricts both to each time slot's single top-ranked matchup, and applies to both Gemini's and your own numbers. "Open" games are picks/predictions made on a game that hasn't finished yet. Every week ever built is included here, not just the current board. "Winnings" treats every graded pick/prediction as a flat $10 bet. For Gemini, that's hypothetical (a $10 bet on whatever Gemini predicted) and honors Best Matchups, Confidence, and the ML/ATS/Both toggle exactly like Gemini's accuracy numbers do. For "My" winnings, it's your own real picks priced at the odds locked in when you made them, and — like My Accuracy — is affected by Best Matchups only, never by Confidence or the ML/ATS/Both toggle, since what a bet pays doesn't depend on how confident Gemini was or which column it's displayed under.
